## Corrupted Bitstream Caption Dataset (CBCD) Construction
### **Bitstream corruption model**
（插入一幅损坏原理的示意图）  
To simulate realistic transmission errors in JPEG bitstreams, we adopt a random bursty bit-flip model parameterized by _p_ and _λ_. Specifically, the bitstream is scanned sequentially, and at each position a burst error is triggered with probability _p_. Once triggered, a contiguous corruption segment is generated whose length _L_ follows a Poisson distribution with mean _λ_. Within each burst segment, bits are flipped with a fixed bit error rate, modeling localized interference caused by channel noise or packet loss. This formulation allows independent control over the frequency of corruption events (_p_) and their severity (_λ_), and naturally degenerates to the error-free case when _p_ equals zero.

潜在问题：

乍一看我们的模型对于Metadata Loss的情形表现很好，这其实是一个不太“合理”的结果，因为jpeg文件头中当然包括了重要信息，比如霍夫曼码表。那为什么我们的模型表现得那么好？其实是因为预训练的时候“偷看”了答案。byteformer预训练的时候整个数据集文件头都是同一个，而现实中jpeg文件的文件头是多种多样的。对于不符合byteformer预训练条件的文件头，我们的模型能力其实会下降很多。究其根本，是因为我们本质上还是依赖byteformer的预训练权重，而对jpeg所有可能的文件头都做训练似乎又是不现实的。所以对于Metadata Loss的情形，我们的模型还有多少优势，这个问题还有待商榷。
